# Failure Modes & Post-Mortem Log

## Error Taxonomy (Prompting, Generation, Assembly)
Classification of failure types across API timeouts, schema violations, and corrupt media.

## Hallucinations & Artifacts
Visual artifacts, anatomy distortion, and audio glitches encountered during generation.

## Temporal & Identity Inconsistencies
Observed drift in subject identity, clothing, or environment across sequential shots.

## Mitigation Strategies & Guardrails
Validation rules, automated retries, and fallback heuristics.

## Incident & Bug Log
Chronological log of pipeline failures, root cause analysis, and fixes implemented.
